🚨 Protect Yourself From Work-From-Home Scams

  • Never pay someone to receive a job offer.
  • Be cautious of employers requesting gift cards, cryptocurrency, or wire transfers.
  • Verify that you are applying through the company's official career website.
  • Be careful with recruiters who only communicate through messaging apps or personal email addresses.
  • Never provide sensitive personal information until you have verified the employer.
  • Research the company before accepting an offer.
  • For non-phone jobs, carefully read the job description to confirm whether calls are required.
